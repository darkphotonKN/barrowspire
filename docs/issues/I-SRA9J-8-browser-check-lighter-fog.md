## Steps

1. **The fog's weight.** On floor 1, the unexplored rooms past a doorway read as layout under a thin
   fog; walked rooms are dimmer than the room I am in and lighter than unexplored ones. Not too
   much fog, the three states still apart.
2. **No fog hugging me.** Standing in a doorway, the room ahead (or the corridor outside) is already
   clear; walking a corridor, the next stretch clears before I step into it.
3. **Never through a wall.** A room behind a wall, with no opening within two steps, stays fogged.
4. **Brighter where I am.** The region I am in reads clearly brighter than the veiled ones; monster
   plates and bars read as before.
5. **Floors 2 and 3.** After climbing, the tower's dark look is back at once, no fog, as before.
6. **Monsters in a room I can now see.** A monster in a room lit from its doorway shows; say whether
   that feels right (B3 is untouched; this is input for it).
7. **Party.** With a second browser, the teammate's reach clears regions on my screen too.
