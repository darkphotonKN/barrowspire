## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Floor 1 is a dungeon of rooms and corridors; start room quiet; switch, door and stairs far and apart; treasure rooms guarded by elites
- [ ] Monsters come round walls by path; none stuck on corners while chasing or going home
- [ ] Walls nearest the camera are low in rooms and corridors; nothing in play is hidden
- [ ] Frame rate holds while walking the whole floor
- [ ] Reconnect mid-floor lands on the floor fully drawn; climbing to floor 2 shows the veil until the new floor is drawn
- [ ] Floors 2 and 3 look and play exactly as before
- [ ] A teammate's monsters appear and disappear off screen only; no replayed deaths or openings
